# LearnForge Policies


# POLICY-01 — Subscription Plans and Billing

LearnForge subscriptions provide access to a changing catalog of eligible courses for the duration of an active subscription. Subscription access is separate from ownership of individually purchased courses.

Subscriptions are billed according to the plan selected during checkout. Available plans may include monthly and annual options, although not every plan is offered in every country. Prices displayed during checkout are the authoritative price for a new subscription.

Your subscription normally renews automatically unless auto-renewal is disabled before the next billing date. Renewal charges are made using the payment method associated with your account.

If a renewal payment fails, LearnForge may retry the payment and may restrict subscription content until payment succeeds. Updating your payment method can restore access after successful processing.

Subscription prices and included courses can change. Changes to recurring prices should be communicated through the applicable account or email notification process.

IMPORTANT: An older version of this article stated that annual subscriptions were billed monthly. That wording is outdated. Annual subscriptions are normally charged according to the annual price shown at checkout.

Refund eligibility is governed by the refund policy applicable to the subscription and the user's jurisdiction.

Last reviewed: February 2026.

# POLICY-02 — Cancellation and Refund Policy

You can cancel an active subscription at any time through Account Settings where self-service cancellation is available. Cancellation normally prevents the next renewal but does not automatically terminate access immediately.

Unless otherwise stated at checkout, users generally retain subscription access until the end of the already-paid billing period. Canceling immediately after a renewal therefore does not necessarily result in an automatic refund.

Refund requests are evaluated separately from cancellation requests. Eligible requests should include the account email and relevant order or transaction information.

For individual course purchases, LearnForge's standard refund period is generally 14 days, subject to course-consumption restrictions and applicable local law. Certain promotional products, bundles, and third-party purchases may have separate terms.

Where a technical problem materially prevented access to purchased content, Support may review an exception even when the normal refund period has passed.

Older help-center documentation referenced a 7-day refund period for all digital products. That article remains accessible in some archived search results but should not be treated as the current standard policy.

Refunds are normally returned to the original payment method. Processing time depends on the payment provider.

Effective date: January 2026.

# POLICY-03 — Accessibility and Inclusive Learning

LearnForge is committed to improving accessibility across its platform and educational content. Standard platform features may include keyboard navigation, captions, transcripts, adjustable playback speed, accessible color contrast, and compatibility with common assistive technologies.

Accessibility varies by course. Instructor-created materials, external links, downloadable resources, and interactive activities may not provide identical levels of accessibility.

Course creators should provide captions or transcripts for newly published video lessons whenever practical. Images containing essential instructional information should include meaningful alternative text where appropriate.

For interactive exercises that cannot be fully accessed using assistive technology, instructors should provide an alternative means of accessing the instructional objective when feasible.

Learners encountering an accessibility barrier can report it through Support. Reports should include the course, lesson, device, browser, and description of the barrier.

LearnForge does not guarantee that every third-party resource linked from a course will meet LearnForge's accessibility standards.

Note: A previous version of the instructor handbook required captions for every video before publication. The current guidance says "whenever practical," but the accessibility team continues to encourage captions for all instructional videos.

Updated: March 2026.

# POLICY-04 — Mobile Application Usage

The LearnForge mobile application allows users to access supported courses on compatible smartphones and tablets. Certain platform functions may be unavailable or behave differently compared with the web version.

Downloaded lessons, where supported, are available only inside the mobile application. They cannot normally be exported as standalone video files.

Users should periodically reconnect to the internet to synchronize progress, verify account status, and receive updated course information.

Application-store purchases may be subject to Apple App Store or Google Play billing rules. Refund requests for these purchases may need to be submitted through the relevant store depending on the transaction.

The mobile application should be kept reasonably up to date. Very old application versions may stop receiving support after platform changes.

Screen sizes and device capabilities differ substantially. A feature available on a tablet may not be available on a smaller phone.

The previous mobile help article recommended downloading lessons over cellular data. This recommendation has been removed because large downloads can consume substantial data allowances.

Current recommendation: use Wi-Fi for large offline downloads where possible.

Last updated: May 2026.

# POLICY-05 — Instructor Content Guidelines

LearnForge instructors are responsible for ensuring that published course materials are accurate, appropriately sourced, and suitable for the intended learner audience.

Course descriptions should clearly communicate prerequisites, expected outcomes, estimated completion time, and any required software or equipment.

Instructors should avoid presenting outdated technical information as current. When a lesson discusses rapidly changing technology, instructors are encouraged to include a publication or update date.

Assessment questions should measure course objectives rather than relying on obscure details unrelated to the learning outcomes.

Instructors should not request sensitive personal information from learners through assignments, discussion boards, or external forms unless there is a legitimate educational requirement and appropriate safeguards.

Copyrighted material should be used only when the instructor has the necessary rights or permission.

LearnForge may review reported content and request corrections, updates, or removal of material that violates platform requirements.

An older instructor guide stated that courses must contain at least five quizzes. This is no longer a universal requirement. Assessment requirements depend on the course category and learning design.

# POLICY-06 — Course Progress and Completion

LearnForge records course progress using activity events generated when learners watch lessons, complete assessments, or perform other supported activities.

Progress may take several seconds to synchronize. Learners should maintain a stable connection when completing important assessments or reaching the end of a lesson.

If multiple browser tabs or devices are used simultaneously, synchronization conflicts may occur in unusual cases. The most recently synchronized state may not always correspond to the learner's intended session.

Course completion requirements are determined by the individual course. Some courses require only lesson completion, while others include quizzes, projects, or minimum assessment scores.

Progress information is associated with the learner's account and is not automatically transferred between separate accounts.

Support may manually review progress when a technical problem is demonstrated. Manual changes are not guaranteed and may require verification of the learner's activity.

Archived documentation previously claimed that progress was saved "instantly." That wording has been replaced with "automatically synchronized" because network conditions can introduce delays.

Reviewed: April 2026.

# POLICY-07 — Account Security

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their LearnForge credentials. Passwords should not be shared with other individuals.

LearnForge Support will never request a user's complete password, payment-card security code, or authentication code through ordinary support communication.

If an account is suspected to be compromised, users should change their password and review recent account activity. Where available, users should also terminate unfamiliar sessions.

Users who registered using a third-party identity provider should secure that provider account as well.

Account recovery may require verification of account ownership. Support may request transaction information or other non-sensitive account details for this purpose.

Accounts suspected of fraudulent activity may be temporarily restricted while an investigation takes place.

Sharing an account with family members or colleagues can result in mixed progress and assessment records and may violate the terms applicable to individual products.

Security-related requests should not include full credit-card numbers, government identification numbers, or passwords in support tickets.

Effective: December 2025.

# POLICY-08 — Family and Organization Accounts

LearnForge offers individual learning accounts and, for selected customers, organization or classroom arrangements.

An individual course purchase normally grants access to the purchaser's account and should not be redistributed among multiple learners.

Organizations may purchase seats that allow multiple named learners to access eligible content. Administrators can manage enrollment depending on the organization plan.

Family plans, when available, may provide separate learner profiles under one billing relationship. Each learner should use their own profile so progress and certificates remain separate.

Transferring an enrollment from one learner to another is generally permitted only under specific circumstances and may require Support or an organization administrator.

If an employee or student leaves an organization, the organization's administrator may remove or reassign the associated seat according to the organization agreement.

Older documentation referred to a universal "five-user family plan." That plan is no longer offered universally, and availability may vary by region.

For questions about organization licensing, contact LearnForge sales or your designated account administrator.

# POLICY-09 — Technical Requirements and Browser Support

LearnForge is designed primarily for modern web browsers. Supported browsers generally include recent versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari.

Browser support does not mean that every device provides identical performance. Interactive simulations, high-resolution video, and browser-based 3D experiences may require additional CPU, GPU, memory, or network capacity.

Users should maintain current browser and operating-system versions where possible. Unsupported legacy browsers may continue to display basic pages but are not guaranteed to function correctly.

Corporate and school networks may restrict video delivery, WebSocket connections, third-party resources, or other services required by interactive courses. Network administrators may need to allow relevant LearnForge domains.

LearnForge Support may request browser version, operating system, device model, network type, and screenshots when troubleshooting.

An older 2024 article listed Internet Explorer as a supported browser. That information is obsolete and should not be used when diagnosing current issues.

Minimum bandwidth recommendations may differ between course types. Video quality can automatically adjust based on network conditions.

Last reviewed: June 2026.

# POLICY-10 — Payments, Chargebacks, and Disputed Transactions

LearnForge uses third-party payment processors for many transactions. Payment processing methods vary by country and product.

A pending card authorization does not necessarily mean that LearnForge received a completed payment. Banks may temporarily reserve funds after a failed transaction.

Users should contact Support before initiating a chargeback when a transaction is recognized but there is a disagreement about access, cancellation, or refund eligibility. Support can review the order and applicable policy.

Unauthorized transactions should also be reported to the user's bank or payment provider through the appropriate fraud-reporting process.

If a chargeback is initiated, LearnForge may receive a dispute notification from the payment processor and may temporarily restrict access associated with the disputed transaction.

Refunds are normally issued to the original payment method where possible.

Support agents must not request complete card numbers, CVV codes, PINs, banking passwords, or authentication codes.

Note: An older internal billing document instructed agents to ask users for the first six and last four digits of their card. That instruction has been retired. Agents should request only the minimum payment information needed for transaction identification.
