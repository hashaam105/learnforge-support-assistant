# LearnForge Sample Knowledge Base

Synthetic support knowledge base for the take-home assignment. Use this as the
corpus for your RAG pipeline.

## Contents
- `faqs.md` — 15 course/product FAQ entries
- `policies.md` — 10 policy / help-center document excerpts
- `tickets.md` — 15 past support ticket transcripts

## Notes
- This data intentionally includes contradictions, outdated policy language,
  and ambiguous tickets — this is realistic and part of the exercise (your
  system should handle stale/conflicting info gracefully).
- Feel free to chunk, embed, or restructure this data however fits your design
  (e.g., splitting each entry into its own chunk/record).
