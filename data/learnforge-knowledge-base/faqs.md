# LearnForge FAQs


# FAQ-01 — How do I access a course after purchasing it?

QUESTION:
I purchased a LearnForge course, but I don't see it in my dashboard. How can I access it?

ANSWER:
After completing a purchase, your course should normally appear under My Learning within a few minutes. Make sure you are signed in with the same email address you used during checkout. If you purchased through Google or Apple sign-in, verify that you are not currently logged in using a different login method associated with another email address.

If the course still does not appear, sign out and sign back in, then refresh the My Learning page. You can also check your purchase confirmation email for the course name and account email.

For purchases made through a mobile app store, access may take slightly longer to synchronize. Do not purchase the course again while troubleshooting, as this can result in duplicate charges.

If the course remains unavailable after 30 minutes, contact Support with your purchase receipt or transaction ID. Support can verify the transaction and manually restore access when appropriate.

# FAQ-02 — Can I get a refund for a course?

QUESTION:
I bought a course yesterday and don't want it anymore. Can I get a refund?

ANSWER:
LearnForge generally allows eligible course purchases to be refunded within 14 days of purchase, provided that the course has not been substantially consumed. For individual courses, "substantially consumed" generally means that a significant portion of the course has already been watched or completed.

Some products have different refund conditions. Promotional bundles, discounted packages, subscriptions, and purchases made through third-party app stores may follow separate terms. If a course was purchased using a promotional voucher, the refund may also be subject to the terms of that promotion.

To request a refund, contact Support using the email associated with your LearnForge account and include your order number. Do not submit multiple refund requests for the same transaction.

Refund decisions are based on the applicable policy at the time of purchase. If you experienced a technical problem that prevented you from using the course, explain the issue in your request even if you are outside the normal refund window. Support may review technical exceptions separately.

# FAQ-03 — Why isn't my course progress being saved?

QUESTION:
I completed several lessons, but my dashboard still shows the old progress.

ANSWER:
LearnForge normally saves lesson progress automatically when you complete or exit a lesson. Progress synchronization requires an active internet connection. If your connection drops while a lesson is ending, the completion event may not reach our servers.

First, reopen the affected lesson and watch or complete the final section again. Leave the lesson normally rather than immediately closing the browser tab. Then return to My Learning and refresh the page.

If you are using multiple devices, allow a few minutes for progress to synchronize before switching devices. Opening the same course simultaneously in several browser tabs can occasionally cause older progress information to overwrite a newer session.

Clearing browser cookies should not normally be necessary. If you continue to experience the problem, provide Support with the course name, lesson title, approximate completion time, and device/browser you were using. Support can check whether the completion event was recorded on the account and restore progress where possible.

# FAQ-04 — What happens if my payment fails?

QUESTION:
My card was declined while purchasing a course. Did LearnForge charge me?

ANSWER:
A declined card payment does not normally create a completed LearnForge order. However, your bank may temporarily place an authorization hold on the amount. This can look like a charge even though LearnForge has not received the payment.

Check your LearnForge account under Orders & Billing to see whether an order was successfully created. You should also receive an order confirmation email for completed purchases.

If there is no completed order but your bank shows a pending transaction, the authorization will usually disappear automatically according to your bank's processing time. LearnForge Support cannot normally release a bank authorization manually.

If you see a completed charge and have not received the course, contact Support with the transaction reference, date, amount, and email address associated with your LearnForge account. Please do not send your full card number or security code.

For recurring subscriptions, a failed renewal may temporarily restrict premium access until a valid payment method is added.

# FAQ-05 — How do I reset my password?

QUESTION:
I forgot my LearnForge password. What should I do?

ANSWER:
Select Forgot password? on the LearnForge sign-in page and enter the email address associated with your account. We will send a password-reset message containing a secure link.

If you do not see the email within several minutes, check your spam, junk, promotions, or filtered folders. Search for "LearnForge" in your mailbox. Make sure you entered the same email address used to create your account.

Password-reset links are temporary and may expire after a limited period. If your link has expired, request a new one rather than repeatedly opening the old link.

If you originally registered using Google, Apple, or another supported social login, you may not have a separate LearnForge password. In that case, use the corresponding social sign-in option.

Support cannot provide your existing password or bypass account security checks. If you no longer have access to the email address on the account, contact Support and provide enough information for us to verify ownership.

# FAQ-06 — When will I receive my certificate?

QUESTION:
I finished all the lessons. Where is my certificate?

ANSWER:
Certificates are issued only for courses that support certificate completion. Completing every video is not necessarily sufficient if the course also contains required quizzes, assignments, or final assessments.

Once all required activities have been completed and your progress has synchronized, the certificate may become available from the course completion page. Some courses require a minimum assessment score before issuing a certificate.

If your certificate is missing, first open the course and check whether any lesson, quiz, or assignment remains incomplete. Refresh your dashboard after completing the remaining requirement.

Certificates normally display the name associated with your LearnForge profile. If your name is incorrect, update your profile before generating the certificate where the course permits this.

Certificates from older courses may use a different layout or may not be automatically generated. If Support confirms that you completed the requirements but cannot locate the certificate, we can investigate the course record and advise whether a certificate can be issued manually.

# FAQ-07 — Can I watch courses offline?

QUESTION:
Can I download LearnForge courses and watch them without an internet connection?

ANSWER:
Offline viewing is available only for selected courses and supported mobile applications. Not every course can be downloaded because instructors or licensing agreements may restrict offline access.

When offline downloads are supported, the course or lesson will display a download option inside the LearnForge mobile application. Downloaded lessons are stored inside the application rather than as ordinary video files that can be copied to another device.

You generally need to connect to the internet periodically so the application can verify your account and synchronize progress. Offline progress may not immediately appear on another device until synchronization occurs.

The desktop web version does not currently provide the same offline-download functionality as the mobile application. Some older help articles refer to a desktop download feature; those instructions may no longer apply.

If a download option is missing, check that your application is updated and that the specific course supports offline viewing.

# FAQ-08 — What devices and browsers are supported?

QUESTION:
Can I use LearnForge on my phone or an older laptop?

ANSWER:
LearnForge courses can generally be accessed through a modern web browser on computers, tablets, and smartphones. Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari are supported, although specific interactive features may behave differently between browsers.

For the best experience, use a current version of your browser and keep your operating system updated. Interactive exercises and higher-resolution video may require more memory or processing power than basic video lessons.

Older devices may still play standard course videos but can experience slow loading, dropped frames, or problems with interactive activities.

LearnForge does not require a special graphics card for ordinary courses. However, some advanced simulations and 3D learning activities have higher hardware requirements, which are listed on the individual course page when applicable.

If you experience problems, try disabling browser extensions, switching browsers, and testing the course in a private/incognito window. If the issue continues, contact Support with your device model, operating system, browser version, and affected course.

# FAQ-09 — Can I change the email address on my account?

QUESTION:
I used my university email when signing up, but I'm graduating. Can I change my account email?

ANSWER:
In most cases, you can update the email address associated with your LearnForge account from Account Settings > Profile. You may be asked to verify both the existing and new email addresses depending on your account status.

Changing your email address does not normally remove your courses, certificates, or historical progress because those records remain associated with your LearnForge account.

If your account was created through an organization or institution, your email address may be controlled by that organization's LearnForge administrator. In that situation, the email-change option may not be available.

If you no longer have access to your old university email, contact Support before creating a second account. Creating another account can result in courses and certificates being split across two profiles.

Provide your current account email, requested new email, and relevant purchase information when contacting Support. Never send your password or full payment-card details.

# FAQ-10 — Can I share my course account with someone else?

QUESTION:
I paid for a course. Can my brother use my account too?

ANSWER:
LearnForge accounts are intended for individual users unless a product specifically provides multi-user or organizational access. Sharing your personal account credentials with another person can cause progress, assessment results, certificates, and recommendations to become mixed together.

Some courses also include assessments that are intended to measure the enrolled learner's own work. Allowing another person to complete those activities can invalidate the results.

For families, schools, or teams that need multiple learners to access the same content, LearnForge may offer family, classroom, or organization plans depending on the product.

If you accidentally shared your password, change it immediately and sign out of other active sessions if that option is available in your account settings.

If multiple people have already used the account, contact Support before purchasing another course. We can explain which account structure is appropriate and whether existing course access can be transferred.

# FAQ-11 — Why is my video not loading?

QUESTION:
The lesson page opens, but the video stays on a loading screen.

ANSWER:
Video playback problems are commonly caused by network restrictions, browser extensions, temporary connectivity problems, or an outdated browser.

Start by refreshing the page and checking whether other websites load normally. If possible, try switching between Wi-Fi and another connection. You can also test the course in an incognito/private browser window.

Ad blockers, privacy extensions, corporate firewalls, and some DNS-filtering services can interfere with video delivery. Temporarily disabling extensions can help identify the cause.

If other LearnForge videos play but one particular lesson does not, the problem may be specific to that lesson rather than your device. Note the course and lesson title and contact Support.

For institutional users, your school or employer may restrict certain video or content-delivery domains. In that case, your network administrator may need to allow LearnForge's required services.

Do not repeatedly purchase the course because of a playback issue. Your existing enrollment remains associated with your account.

# FAQ-12 — Are course prices refundable after a discount?

QUESTION:
I bought a course at full price and then saw a 40% discount the next day. Can you refund the difference?

ANSWER:
LearnForge promotions can change without advance notice, and seeing a lower promotional price after purchase does not automatically qualify an order for a partial refund.

Some limited promotions may include price-adjustment terms, while others specifically exclude previous purchases. The applicable promotional terms are normally shown on the offer page.

If the difference appeared shortly after your purchase, you can contact Support with your order number and the promotion you saw. Support can determine whether that particular promotion included a price-adjustment provision.

Please do not cancel and repurchase solely to obtain a discount before Support reviews the situation. A new purchase may be subject to different terms and can complicate course progress, certificates, or promotional eligibility.

This FAQ concerns price differences, not technical problems or unauthorized charges. If you believe your account was charged twice, report the duplicate transactions separately so they can be investigated as a billing issue.

# FAQ-13 — What happens when my subscription expires?

QUESTION:
Will I lose the courses I completed if my subscription expires?

ANSWER:
Your completed learning history and account generally remain available after a subscription expires. However, access to subscription-included courses may end when the subscription period ends.

Courses purchased separately are generally not affected by subscription expiration, although promotional or bundled products may have different terms.

Your progress data is retained according to LearnForge's account and data-retention practices. If you later reactivate an eligible subscription, previously recorded progress may become available again for courses that are still included in the subscription.

Certificates already earned are generally retained in your account even if you no longer have an active subscription.

If you believe you lost access to a course that you purchased separately, check your Orders & Billing history before purchasing it again. Contact Support if the purchase is visible but access is missing.

# FAQ-14 — Can I get accessibility support?

QUESTION:
Does LearnForge support captions and screen readers?

ANSWER:
LearnForge aims to make course content accessible across supported devices. Many courses include closed captions, transcripts, adjustable playback controls, keyboard navigation, and compatibility with common screen-reading software.

Accessibility support can vary between courses because instructors may provide additional materials independently of the LearnForge platform. Interactive simulations may also have limitations that do not apply to standard video lessons.

If captions are available, you can normally enable them using the CC button in the video player. Some courses provide downloadable transcripts or text alternatives from the resources section.

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, contact Support and describe the specific activity, device, browser, and assistive technology involved. Where possible, Support can provide an alternative resource or escalate the issue to the content team.

We also encourage learners to report inaccessible content even when they are able to complete the course, as reports help identify areas for improvement.

# FAQ-15 — I don't recognize a LearnForge charge. What should I do?

QUESTION:
There is a LearnForge charge on my credit card, but I don't remember making the purchase.

ANSWER:
First, check your LearnForge account's Orders & Billing section and search your email for a LearnForge receipt. The transaction may have been made through a subscription renewal, family member's account, or another email address.

If you still do not recognize the charge, contact Support promptly with the transaction date, amount, currency, and the last four digits of the payment card if needed for identification. Do not send the full card number, CVV, PIN, or banking password.

If the payment was made through Apple, Google, PayPal, or another external payment provider, the provider may need to handle the dispute depending on how the transaction was processed.

LearnForge can investigate whether a transaction corresponds to an account or order. If you believe your payment method was used without authorization, you should also follow your bank or payment provider's fraud-reporting procedure.
